## HSD Team Name: "Null Pointer Exception", Number: 

Welcome to my respository for Gunter's Blacksmith Storefront Application, developed for the **2026 KU High School Design Competition under Computer Science**.

In a medieval era, this application transforms magical orbs into a modern e-commerce shop to help manage orders. Similarly to Amazon's customer webpage.

---

## Project Overview
Gunter's blacksmith shop has experienced difficulty managing incoming orders, including items such as swords and armor. Utilizing the magical orb within Mautern, we can create a mobile web application to optimize the storefront experience.

Customers can browse the selection of weaponry, place their custom order directly, and track their order's position on Gunter's to-do list.

### Built with:
*   **HTML5**   -   Application structure and skeleton.
*   **CSS**    -   Custom styling and responsively layout.
*   **JavaScript**  -   Establish logic, tracking, and functionality.
*   **JSON**    -   Stores lists of items and additional information.

---

## Usage Guide
1.  **Browsing:** To browse items, select what category of items you'd like to order (default to swords) which will display a selection of picks.
2.  **Details:** Clicking on the item or anywhere near it will present details of the items.
3.  **Ordering:** To order, click on the button that says "Add to Cart," including the quantity (default to 1).
4.  **Queue:** To search for the live to-do list, look towards the top of the webpage for a "To-Do List" option which should display your active orders, including list of items, quantities, Order ID, Time, Customer Name, Note, etc.
6.  **Verbal Order:** To verbally order your item, click on the button above the categories, then (using a microphone for desktop) verbally communicate your order. There should be a helper element to provide an example, and an additional option to type your order. After pressing "Done," it'll display your order prompting if it's correct, press "Yes" to add to your cart.

## Feature List

### Core Features:
*   **Interactive Shop:** Optimized interface modeled similar to modern e-commerce stores like Amazon.
*   **Order Placement:** Checkout functionality for items including name and optional details.
*   **Live Queue Position:** Dynamic order tracking that places customer orders in Gunter's to-do list.

### Custom Features:
*   **Audible Ordering:** Linked audio input to customer order. Customers may verbally say what items to order and quantity.
